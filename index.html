The Philippines: Between Alliances and Regional Tensions

Emerging Nations

Dive into the heart of the Philippines with this exclusive issue, crafted in collaboration with Hubert Testard (Professor at Sciences Po) and with the support of the Embassy of the Philippines.

In this special report, we move beyond the usual headlines to uncover a nation in transition. From high-potential agri-tech and untapped eco-tourism to industrial ambitions and a hands-on diplomatic presence, the country is laying the groundwork for a development model on its own terms.

Political Crises in Latin America

Emerging Nations

The Núcleo Milenio CRISPOL publishes its first regional analysis bulletin dedicated to the major political crises and democratic tensions that marked Latin America in 2025.

Researchers from several countries examine power dynamics and institutional fragilities through five case studies: Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, and Peru.
